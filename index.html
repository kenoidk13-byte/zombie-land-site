what was

More than a portfolio, this became a curated visual space with better pacing, stronger atmosphere, and a clearer point of view — straight from the grave.

Overview

I created these characters because I really love zombies and everything related to them. I like their unusual and creepy appearance. I wanted to create my own zombies with interesting designs. I hope they turned out unusual and memorable.

My Approach

When creating the zombies, I was inspired by movies, games, and other works about zombies. I was inspired by their scary and unusual appearance. I also added my own ideas to make the characters look original. Most of all, I wanted to create a dark and frightening atmosphere.

Outcome

15 undead assets delivered, each one ready to crawl off the screen and bite. The result feels sharper, more controlled, and impossible to walk past.

Vinogradov Vladimir — Art Direction
"The final result feels far more resolved than what we started with — there's more atmosphere, more gore."
